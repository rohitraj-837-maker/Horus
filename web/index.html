What you are looking at

  1. Dots are airliners at the selected altitude. A ringed dot is one whose own vertical-speed data shows turbulence right now.
  2. Orange cells are the forecast: the chance that the next aircraft through that area in the next 15 min meets turbulence. Brighter = more likely.
  3. Carets ^ are pilot reports of turbulence (moderate, severe).

Live replays a real archived day (2026-01-01) at 30× speed, because free live feeds are too sparse for the detector. Research prototype, not for flight decisions.
