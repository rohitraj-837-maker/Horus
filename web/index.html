Altitude

Every airliner already measures turbulence: its vertical speed jitters when the air is rough. Horus reads that jitter from public ADS-B broadcasts and turns it into a 15-minute forecast map.

Dots are aircraft at the selected altitude; orange means its own data shows turbulence now. Shaded cells are the forecast chance that the next aircraft through meets turbulence. ^ marks a pilot report. Click an aircraft to follow it.
