The key idea

Rounding looks like turbulence. So we take it out.

ADS-B reports vertical rate in steps of 64 ft/min. That rounding adds jitter of its own; Sheppard's correction subtracts exactly that amount.

σ²corr = σ²raw − Δ²/12  ·  Δ = 64 ft/min
