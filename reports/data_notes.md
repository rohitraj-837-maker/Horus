**Consequence:** the SPEC's 5–10 s live snapshot rate cannot be reached on any free source. Live per-aircraft cadence is about **90 s**. The detector-rate benchmarks (1 s / 5 s / 10 s) need full-rate data (Trino) and are produced by downsampling it. Live mode will run at 90 s, so a 90 s row is added to the rate benchmarks.

The recorder paces itself from `x-rate-limit-remaining` so credits last until the 00:00 UTC reset (`live.opensky_interval`).

## PIREPs (IEM archive + aviationweather.gov)

- IEM CSV contains stray `"` characters (e.g. `/TB "SO FAR SO GOOD"`), which break standard CSV parsing. The parser ignores quoting and splits the 5 clean trailing fields from the end.
- The same PIREP is reissued in several products (KMSC + KWBC). Dedupe on time, position, aircraft, altitude and intensity removes about 4% of rows.
- Turbulence text is free-form (`LT CHP`, `MD TURB`, `LIGHT TO MOD`, `MOD090`, `CONS LGT OCNL MOD`). Parse failure rate (has a `/TB` group but no intensity word): **0.4%** (2026-11-01), **1.0%** (2026-11-14).
- Includes AIREP-format reports with no slash groups (`... F360 ... TB NONE`); these are parsed too.
- **Mixed-intensity rule.** AWC's own decoder is inconsistent: `CONS LGT OCNL MOD` becomes LGT-MOD, while `OCNL LGT INTMT MOD` becomes LGT. We store both `tb_max` and `tb_min`. MOD+ defaults to `tb_max ≥ MOD`, and the `tb_min` rule gets its own benchmark row.

| UTC file | Reports | With TB | MOD+ (max rule) | MOD+ (min rule) | LGT+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-01 | 4,783 | 2,730 | 1,062 | 752 | 2,142 |
| 2026-11-14 | 2,768 | 1,215 | 345 | 255 | 886 |

PIREPs are biased toward bumpy air (MOD+ is 28–39% of reports that mention turbulence), so they can't be used as a base rate. Absence of a PIREP is weak evidence (SPEC §6.1).

## Aircraft types

- OpenSky aircraft DB: 519,969 airframes, **last published 2024-11** (2 years stale). ICAO Doc 8643: 2,640 designators. It has no `J` (super) category, so A388/A225 are mapped to `super` manually.
- Coverage on the live archive: **72.3%** of airborne aircraft at FL200 or above resolve to a typecode. Size-class mix: medium_jet 57%, UNK 28%, heavy 10%, light 4%.

## Vertical-rate quantisation (SPEC open question 1)

`state_vectors` / REST `vertical_rate` is in **m/s with 2 decimals**. Converted to ft/min, every ADS-B value lies within 1 ft/min of a multiple of 64. **The 64 ft/min step survives OpenSky's processing.** Sheppard's correction Δ²/12 = 341.3 (ft/min)² applies.

## adsb.lol daily history archives (candidate full-rate substitute)

- GitHub releases `adsblol/globe_history_2025` and `_2026`, one per day, about **3.5 GB/day** (global, two tar parts), **ODbL-1.0**. They cover winter 2025–26 (the jet-stream season in SPEC §3).
- Format: readsb `traces/xx/trace_full_<icao>.json` (gzip). Each point is `[dt, lat, lon, alt, gs, track, flags, baro_rate, {msg}, type, geom_alt, …]`, with typecode in the file header.
- Measured on 186 aircraft at FL200 or above (2025-12-30, 169k points): gap percentiles 10/25/50/75/90 % = **1.7 / 5.8 / 16.2 / 19.7 / 19.9 s**. Only 9% of gaps are 1.5 s or less. 92.8% of vertical rates are on the 64 ft/min grid.
- **Not 1 Hz.** readsb thins steady flight to about 20 s and adds points when values change, so sampling is denser when vertical rate jumps. This sampling depends on the signal and would bias variance statistics if treated as uniform.
